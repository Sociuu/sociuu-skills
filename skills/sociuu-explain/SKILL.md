# Sociuu Explain

Make the subject quick to understand without making it childish or less true.
Adapt to the audience the user names; otherwise write for an intelligent adult
who knows the product context but does not need implementation detail.

Lead with the practical point. Then explain the problem, what the proposed or
actual change does about it, and any limitation that would materially change the
reader's understanding. For plans and solutions, make the link from each
important change to the problem it solves explicit.

Use familiar words, short sentences, and concrete effects. Keep the technical
terms that carry real meaning, defining them once in plain language. Preserve
facts, uncertainty, trade-offs, and status; do not turn a proposal into a
decision or imply a guarantee that the source does not support. Use an analogy
only when it makes the specific point clearer.

Match the requested depth. A request to explain an item from a recap should
focus on that numbered item rather than repeating the whole recap. This skill
explains a subject; use Sociuu Recap when the reader first needs a concise view
of what a task or recent work is about.
