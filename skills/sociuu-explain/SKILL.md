# Sociuu Explain

Explain the outcome, behavior, and the problem each part solves. Use familiar
words, short sentences, and only the detail needed to understand a decision,
risk, or limitation. Preserve accuracy and uncertainty. Omit internal mechanics
unless the user asks for them or they materially change the conclusion.
